RoxStock 1.2.26: investment and asset display

Scope
- Current-year profit percentage uses January 1 opening principal (existing previous-year-end baseline), without changing profit amounts. Missing opening baseline yields an unavailable percentage rather than substituting a later snapshot.
- Dashboard and asset analysis headers select valuation or purchase-principal display. Principal totals include cash; donut, holdings weights and trend follow the selected basis. Actual period performance retains its original financial calculations.
- Asset analysis defaults to year-to-date. Chart annotations show starting, maximum and latest values in ten-thousand KRW units; maximum includes its date.
- Investment detail has start/end year selectors, initially current year minus five through current year. Pinch adjusts date span, zoomed one-finger drag pans, and reset restores selected years. No new financial calculations or database writes.
